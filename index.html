Who We Are

A New Era of Coaching

Sri Krishna Academy was founded with a singular, unwavering mission — to make world-class government exam coaching accessible to every deserving student across West Bengal. Born in Hariharpara, we are committed to bringing the highest standard of preparation to our community.

We believe every student carries the potential to serve their nation through a government role. Our purpose is to unlock that potential through structured learning, meticulous guidance, and relentless encouragement at every step.

With a team of 10+ expert faculty, modern infrastructure, and a student-first approach — we are ready to uphold our promise every single day: "তোমার স্বপ্ন, আমাদের প্রতিশ্রুতি."

Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality coaching that equips every student with the knowledge, skills, and confidence needed to crack competitive government examinations. We are committed to an inclusive learning environment where every student, regardless of background, gets a fair chance to achieve their dreams.

🌟 Our Vision

To be West Bengal's most trusted government job coaching institute — producing not just successful candidates, but responsible officers who strengthen the nation. We envision a future where every Sri Krishna Academy student becomes a beacon of excellence and service in their field.
